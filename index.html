Job search mail helper

Job search mail helper is a small personal tool. Its owner uses it to send his own job-application emails from his own Gmail account, with his CV attached as a PDF file.

  • Only the owner uses it. It is not offered to other people.
  • It can only send email. It cannot read, change or delete anything in the mailbox.
  • It asks Google for three permissions: send email on your behalf (gmail.send), and openid and email, so it can check that the right account signed in.
